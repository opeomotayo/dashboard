Privacy Policy

Dashboard · opeomotayo.github.io/dashboard · Last updated 20 November 2026

Dashboard is a set of three personal web pages — the Goal Console, the Budget Console and the Property Console — written and used by Ope Omotayo. They run entirely in your browser. There is no server behind them, no account to create, and no analytics.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the pages served at https://opeomotayo.github.io/dashboard/ and to the Google OAuth client those pages use to reach Google Drive. The developer and sole operator is Ope Omotayo, who can be reached at ope.omotayo@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you type into a console — goals, budget lines, properties, costs, document records — is written to IndexedDB in your own browser. That copy never leaves your device unless you connect Google Drive.

If you connect Google Drive, the app additionally keeps:

  • One JSON file per console in your Drive, holding that console’s records, so the same data opens on your other devices.
  • Any document you upload through the Property Console’s Documents page, filed under a folder the app creates called Property Console Documents, then a folder per property, then a folder per kind of paperwork.

These files live in your Google Drive, under your own Google account. They are not copied anywhere else.

What Google user data the app accesses

The app requests exactly one OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — permission to create files and folders in your Google Drive, and to read and change only those the app itself created.

This is Google’s per-file scope. The app cannot see the rest of your Drive: files you created elsewhere, or that others shared with you, are invisible to it. The app does not request your name, email address, profile, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data.

How that data is used

Solely to provide the features you asked for: to save your console data so it survives a reload and reaches your other devices, and to store the documents you choose to upload. The access token Google issues is held in memory for the length of the browser session and is not written down anywhere.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. Your data is not transmitted to the developer, to any server operated by the developer, or to any third party. There is no backend to receive it. The only network requests the pages make are to Google’s own APIs on your behalf, and to Google Fonts for typefaces.

The app does not use your data for advertising, does not sell or transfer it to data brokers, and does not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Google Workspace APIs are not used to develop, improve, or train generalised or non-personalised AI and/or ML models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How it is protected

The pages are served over HTTPS. Data at rest in Google Drive is protected by your own Google account and whatever sign-in protections you have on it. Data in your browser is protected by your device and browser profile. Because no copy reaches a server operated by the developer, there is no developer-side database to breach.

Retention and deletion

  • In your browser: use Wipe database at the foot of any console, or clear site data for this site in your browser settings.
  • In your Drive: delete the console’s JSON file, or the Property Console Documents folder, from Google Drive like any other file. Removing a document inside the app moves the Drive file to your Drive bin.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app keeps no copy, so nothing of yours remains with the developer.

Cookies and tracking

The app sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking pixels or advertising scripts. It uses your browser’s local storage only to remember your light or dark theme choice, and IndexedDB for the data described above. Google’s sign-in library may set cookies on Google’s own domains when you sign in; that is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

Children

These are personal record-keeping tools and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the way the app handles your data changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. The page history is public in the repository.
